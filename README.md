# dataflo.studio

### Turn your business data into better decisions.

Running a business generates a lot of data. But are you actually using it to make better decisions?

Whether you're trying to grow revenue, reduce operational costs, automate repetitive tasks or simply understand what's happening in your business, I'd love to help.

**Here's how I can help:**

- **Business intelligence** — Unify data from different sources (Excel, payment platforms, etc) to give you a clearer picture of your business.
  
- **Data analytics** — Understand your customers, revenue streams, business performance and identify opportunities for improvement.
  
- **Workflow automation** — Eliminate repetitive manual processes and spend less time moving data around.
  
- **Forecasting & machine learning** — Explore predictive analytics to anticipate trends and make more informed business decisions.
  
- **Technical training** — Learn practical Excel, SQL, Python and reporting skills that you can apply directly to your work.
